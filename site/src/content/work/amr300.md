## What I did

- Designed and developed the drive system together with the engineering team.
- Integrated the ROS2 navigation stack with the low-level motor controllers, interfacing ROS2 with an ESP32 over serial to drive the hub servo motors.
- Tested and validated the 300 kg payload capacity under real-world conditions.

## What I learned

Cross-functional teamwork with hardware and software engineers, debugging in a real production environment, and technical documentation and progress reporting.
